The Poetry

Words for the moments we cannot explain.

Love

Where You Are

"I found a little piece of home in the sound of your name..."

I found a little piece of home in the sound of your name. A quiet place inside my heart where nothing stays the same. And when the world becomes too loud, I think of you once more— For somehow every road I take still leads me to your door.
Life

Keep Walking

"The road does not promise easy days, only another sunrise..."

The road does not promise easy days, only another sunrise. So carry your scars, carry your dreams, and keep walking. There are flowers you have not seen, and skies you have not yet watched turn gold.
Nature

After Rain

"The earth remembers how to breathe after the rain..."

The earth remembers how to breathe after the rain. Leaves hold little mirrors of the morning sky. The wind becomes softer, the streets become silver, and for a moment the whole world feels new again.
Dreams

Someday

"Someday the things you dream about will become memories..."

Someday the things you dream about will become memories. The places you imagined will have roads beneath your feet. So dream without apology. The future has room for every beautiful thing you dare to believe.
Night

Moonlight

"The moon never asks the night to be anything but dark..."

The moon never asks the night to be anything but dark. It simply shines, quietly reminding us that even darkness can hold a little light.
Hope

Begin Again

"There is no shame in beginning again..."

There is no shame in beginning again. A broken heart can still learn to sing. A tired soul can still find morning. And a life can change direction with one brave step.
